> FRAGMENT 4 — RECIPE

Recipe for Nipa

Prep time: 17 years. Serves: one person, eventually.

Ingredients

— 1 childhood that ended early
— A generous handful of silence
— A camera, film preferred
— 3 cups of noticing what other people miss
— 1 tablespoon of stubbornness
— Fresh flowers, still on the branch
— A pigeon, for company
— A brother from a different timeline
— Salt, to taste

Method

1. Take the childhood. Don't cut off the hard parts. They're the flavor base.

2. Simmer in silence over low heat until everyone else in the house has gone.

3. While it reduces, notice things. The bench with petals on it. The pigeon on the rock. The light through the bougainvillea. Photograph all of them.

4. Add stubbornness. Stir counterclockwise. Do not let it burn.

5. Somewhere along the way, drop in a brother you didn't know you had. Same age as you. Different universe. He'll argue with this step. Ignore him.

6. Serve warm. Do not refrigerate. Does not keep. Best eaten immediately.

Serving Suggestion

Eat on a porch. Alone, or with someone who doesn't ask questions. Follow with tea. Follow the tea with a walk. Follow the walk with more tea.

This dish is not for everyone. It's for the people who get it.

Chef's Note

I've never made this. I've only watched it being made. From a distance. Through a screen. That counts, I think. That counts.

— M
